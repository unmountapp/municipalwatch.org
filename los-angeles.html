Sample brief · Los Angeles, CA

What LA City Planning is hearing next

This is the format of a Municipal Watch weekly brief: each item, its date, what it means for your business, and a link to the official source.

Sample prepared November 23, 2026 from official City of Los Angeles Department of City Planning hearing notices for Dec 1 to Dec 16, 2026. City Council and City Planning Commission meetings this week are recessed or canceled for Thanksgiving. Hearings can be continued or changed; always confirm against the linked source.

Dec 1, 2026Zoning Administrator hearing, 10:00 a.m., virtual (Case ZA-2026-4306-CUB, Council District 9)

1. South LA: El Super grocery seeks a full liquor license at 446 E. Washington Blvd

What's happening: Chedraui USA (El Super) asks for a conditional use permit to sell a full line of alcohol for off-site consumption at a proposed 44,450 sq ft full-service grocery store in an existing commercial building. Proposed hours are 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. daily.

Why it matters: Nearby grocers, liquor stores and corner markets: a large new competitor is opening. Landlords on Washington Blvd: a big grocery anchor usually lifts foot traffic. Neighbors can comment at the hearing.

Source: LA City Planning hearing notice, Dec 1, 2026 (ZA-2026-4306-CUB)
Dec 7, 2026Hearing Officer, 11:00 a.m. and 11:30 a.m., virtual (Cases DIR-2026-903 and DIR-2025-6810, Council District 11)

2. Venice: two new retail and live/work buildings on Abbot Kinney with zero parking

What's happening: Two coastal permit hearings for 1209 and 1231 Abbot Kinney Blvd. Each would replace a one-story house with a two-story mixed-use building of about 4,300 sq ft: roughly 2,800 sq ft of ground-floor retail plus one live/work unit, with no on-site parking. Each also asks for zero side-yard setbacks.

Why it matters: Abbot Kinney retailers and landlords: about 5,600 sq ft of new shop space is coming to the street. Brokers: watch for new leasing supply. Neighbors: no new parking is planned.

Source: LA City Planning hearing notice, Dec 7, 2026 (DIR-2026-903-CDP-ADJ-MEL-SPPC)
Dec 9, 2026Deputy Advisory Agency and Hearing Officer, 10:30 a.m., virtual (VTT-74415-CN, CPC-2016-3341, Council District 11)

3. Del Rey: Paseo Marina would replace a mall with 425 apartments and offices

What's happening: The Paseo Marina project at 13400–13450 Maxella Ave and 4305–4363 Glencoe Ave would demolish a 100,781 sq ft shopping mall and build 425 homes (10% for very low-income households), 90,000 sq ft of office, 20,000 sq ft of retail and 20,000 sq ft of restaurant space in four buildings up to 79 feet. It asks for a zone change and a permit for alcohol sales at up to 12 establishments. The Final EIR was due Nov 13.

Why it matters: Current mall tenants: plan for relocation. Nearby restaurants and shops: new residents and office workers, but also new competition. Brokers and property managers near Marina del Rey: 425 new rental units will add supply.

Source: LA City Planning hearing notice, Dec 9, 2026 (Paseo Marina Project)
Dec 15, 2026Zoning Administrator hearing, 10:00 a.m., virtual (Case ZA-2026-3606-CUB, Council District 10)

4. Koreatown: new 59-seat restaurant at 3751 W. 6th St seeks a full liquor license until 2 a.m.

What's happening: The applicant asks to sell a full line of alcohol for on-site drinking at a proposed 3,376 sq ft, 59-seat restaurant with DJ performances. Proposed hours are 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. daily. It also asks for relief from the hours limits that apply to mini-shopping centers and commercial corners.

Why it matters: Koreatown bars and restaurants: another late-night venue is coming to 6th and Harvard. Nearby apartment owners and managers: late hours can mean noise complaints, so this is the time to comment.

Source: LA City Planning hearing notice, Dec 15, 2026 (ZA-2026-3606-CUB)
Dec 16, 2026Hearing Officer and Deputy Advisory Agency, 10:30 a.m., virtual (CPC-2024-6371-DB-PR-HCA, VTT-84555-CN, Council District 9)

5. Near USC: 209 units at 3822 S. Figueroa St would replace eight older buildings

What's happening: A new seven-story, 86-foot mixed-use building with 209 homes (42 set aside for low, very low and extremely low-income households) and 2,705 sq ft of ground-floor retail and restaurant space. It would demolish eight multifamily buildings, six of them contributors to the Flower Drive Historic District. The project uses a 32% density bonus. The Final EIR is due Dec 4.

Why it matters: Student-housing owners and property managers near USC: 209 new units will compete for tenants. Tenants in the eight buildings: watch for relocation notices. Preservation groups: this is the hearing to weigh in on the historic district.

Source: LA City Planning hearing notice, Dec 16, 2026 (3822 South Figueroa Project)
