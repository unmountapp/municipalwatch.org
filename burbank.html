Sample brief · Burbank, CA

What Burbank City Hall is deciding next

This is the format of a Municipal Watch weekly brief: each item, its date, what it means for your business, and a link to the official source.

Sample prepared November 27, 2026 from the City of Burbank's posted Dec 3 BWP Board agenda (official Granicus portal) and the City Clerk's official agenda forecast for Dec 8 and Dec 15. The Dec 1 Council meeting is dark. Items marked "confirm on final agenda" are scheduled but not yet on a posted agenda.

Dec 3, 2026BWP Board, 5 pm

1. Electric and water rate change set for January 2027

What's happening: Burbank Water and Power staff will present the upcoming rate adjustment set for January 2027 and how BWP plans to tell customers about it. This is a presentation, not a vote. The City's agenda forecast then lists a Dec 15 Council resolution to update BWP rates and fees in the citywide fee schedule (confirm on final agenda).

Why it matters: Restaurants, breweries, post-production houses, manufacturers and landlords who pay common-area utilities: this is the first public look at what your BWP bills may do in January. Written comments go to BWPBoard@burbankca.gov by noon on Dec 3.

Source: BWP Board agenda, Dec 3, 2026
Dec 15, 2026City Council (forecast), confirm on final agenda

2. Possible urgency ban on standalone data centers

What's happening: The City Clerk's agenda forecast lists Council consideration of regulatory options for standalone data centers, including introduction of an uncodified ordinance and adoption of an uncodified urgency ordinance to prohibit data centers. An urgency ordinance can take effect right away.

Why it matters: Industrial and Media District property owners, brokers and developers: if adopted, a site you hoped to sell or lease for a data center may not be allowed. Owners in talks with data center users should watch this date.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast (City Clerk), Dec 15, 2026
Dec 15, 2026City Council (forecast), confirm on final agenda

3. One-year discount on film permit application fees

What's happening: The forecast lists a Police Department item on a potential one-year Film Permit Application Fee Discount Program.

Why it matters: Production companies, location managers, stage and equipment rental firms: filming in Burbank could get cheaper for a year. Landlords and businesses that host shoots may see more requests.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast (City Clerk), Dec 15, 2026
Dec 8, 2026City Council (forecast), confirm on final agenda

4. Final step for the 420-room hotel at 2500 N. Hollywood Way

What's happening: The forecast lists adoption of the ordinance approving the Planned Development, Development Agreement and Development Review for 2500 North Hollywood Way, on the consent calendar. That is the site of the proposed Aloft/Residence Inn hotel and valet garage next to the existing Marriott.

Why it matters: Hotels, restaurants, airport parking operators and nearby landlords: adoption would lock in the approvals. Contractors and suppliers: this is the step before permits and construction bids.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast (City Clerk), Dec 8, 2026
Dec 8, 2026City Council (forecast), confirm on final agenda

5. Mid-year changes to BWP's utility service rules

What's happening: The forecast lists approval of mid-year updates to the Rules and Regulations Governing Utility Service from Burbank Water and Power.

Why it matters: Developers, electricians and contractors: these rules cover things like new service connections and customer obligations. Property managers: they can change how service, deposits or disconnections work. Check the staff report when the agenda posts.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast (City Clerk), Dec 8, 2026
