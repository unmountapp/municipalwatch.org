Sample brief · Burbank, CA

What Burbank City Council is deciding next

This is the format of a Municipal Watch weekly brief: each item, its date, what it means for your business, and a link to the official source.

Sample prepared October 18, 2026 from the City of Burbank's official agenda forecast. Forecast items can change; always confirm against the final agenda.

Oct 27, 2026City Council (joint meeting), consent calendar

1. Funding agreement with LA Metro for the Downtown Burbank Sidewalk Enhancement Project

What's happening: Council is set to approve a $6,220,000 Measure M funding agreement with Metro covering early planning, environmental review, design and construction of downtown sidewalk work.

Why it matters: Downtown restaurants, shops and landlords: this moves the sidewalk project closer to construction. Expect future work that can affect storefront access, outdoor dining and parking. Now is the time to ask the city about timing and phasing.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast
Nov 10, 2026City Council study session

2. Discussion on a Development Streamlining and Incentives Program

What's happening: Council will hold a study session on a program to streamline development and offer incentives.

Why it matters: Developers, contractors and property owners: a study session is where the shape of a program gets set before any vote. If faster approvals or incentives would change your plans, this is the meeting to watch or speak at.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast
Nov 10, 2026City Council public hearing

3. 2026 Media District Specific Plan Update

What's happening: Council is scheduled to certify the Final Environmental Impact Report, amend the Burbank2035 General Plan, adopt the 2026 Media District Specific Plan Update (with the Sustainable Infrastructure Alternative as the preferred project) and introduce an ordinance changing the zoning code to put it in place. The ordinance is forecast for adoption on Nov 17.

Why it matters: Property owners, investors and businesses in the Media District: this rewrites the rules for what can be built and how land can be used around you, which can affect property values, redevelopment options and nearby construction.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast
Nov 10, 2026City Council report

4. Proposed agreement with IKE Smart City for interactive digital wayfinding kiosks

What's happening: Council will consider the terms of an agreement for IKE Smart City, LLC to install, run and maintain interactive digital wayfinding kiosks.

Why it matters: Retail, restaurant and visitor-facing businesses: these kiosks steer foot traffic and often carry advertising. Worth knowing where they would go and whether local businesses can be listed.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast
Nov 17, 2026City Council public hearing

5. Planned development, development agreement and development review at 2500 North Hollywood Way

What's happening: Council is scheduled to certify a Final Environmental Impact Report (with a statement of overriding considerations) and introduce an ordinance approving a planned development, development agreement and development review for 2500 North Hollywood Way.

Why it matters: Nearby businesses, landlords and contractors: a large project is up for approval. That can mean construction traffic, new neighbors and customers, and bidding opportunities for local trades.

Source: City of Burbank agenda forecast
