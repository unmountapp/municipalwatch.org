Sample brief · Long Beach, CA

What Long Beach City Hall is deciding next

This is the format of a Municipal Watch weekly brief: each item, its date, what it means for your business, and a link to the official source.

Sample prepared October 27, 2026 from the City of Long Beach's official agendas (PrimeGov), staff reports and public hearing notices. Items can change; always confirm against the final agenda.

Oct 27, 2026Housing and Public Health Committee

1. Just cause protections and "substantial remodel" reform

What's happening: The committee is scheduled to hear two presentations: one from Long Beach Residents Empowered (LiBRE) on a LAANE report focused on just cause protection and substantial remodel reform, and one from the Apartment Association, California Southern Cities on rental housing provider perspectives and substantial remodel standards.

Why it matters: Landlords and property managers: "substantial remodel" is one of the grounds for ending a tenancy. A committee discussion like this often comes before proposed rule changes, so it shows where the city may be heading.

Source: Housing and Public Health Committee agenda, Oct 27, 2026
Oct 27, 2026Cultural Heritage Commission

2. Study session on changes to the Mills Act program

What's happening: The commission will hold a study session on the Mills Act Program to evaluate possible future changes, including to application requirements and how the program is run.

Why it matters: Owners of historic homes and buildings, and the agents who sell them: Mills Act contracts can lower property taxes on qualifying historic properties. Changes to how you apply could affect future applications and the value of eligible properties.

Source: Cultural Heritage Commission agenda, Oct 27, 2026
Nov 5, 2026Planning Commission public hearing, 5:00 PM

3. Bigger hangar and office at 3605 E. Spring St. (Long Beach Airport)

What's happening: Bonseph Long Beach, LLC is asking to modify an approved site plan on the airfield: Hangar 1 would grow from 31,500 to 61,695 sq. ft. and Office 1 from a 6,000 sq. ft. one-story office to an 18,265 sq. ft. two-story office. Parking would drop from 117 to 84 stalls and total building area would rise from 74,000 to 116,771 sq. ft. (Case 2603-91, MOD26-004, District 5.)

Why it matters: Aviation businesses, nearby commercial landlords and contractors: more hangar and office space at the airport adds competition and tenants, and a larger build means more construction work nearby.

Source: Planning Commission notice of public hearing
Nov 9, 2026Zoning Administrator hearing, 2:00 PM

4. Recording studio at 1487 Cota Ave., plus a garage and ADU project in Bluff Park

What's happening: The Zoning Administrator will hear an Administrative Use Permit for a 4,050 sq. ft. multi-room rehearsal and recording studio (not open to the public, no live entertainment) in an existing building at 1487 Cota Ave. in the General Industrial zone (Case 2609-14, District 7). The same hearing covers a coastal permit for a new 909 sq. ft. four-car garage at 2515 E. Ocean Blvd., with an ADU above it processed separately (Case 2603-78, District 2).

Why it matters: Industrial landlords and creative businesses: this shows what uses the city will allow in industrial buildings. Homeowners and builders: the Ocean Blvd. case is a live example of garage-plus-ADU projects in a historic district.

Source: Zoning Administrator public hearing notices
Nov 10, 2026City Council public hearing (confirm on final agenda)

5. Zaferia business assessment for 2026-27

What's happening: On Oct 20, Council approved the Zaferia Parking and Business Improvement Area annual report and set a public hearing for Nov 10 on levying the yearly assessment. Rates are unchanged: for example, retail, entertainment and banking businesses pay a $375 base fee, and service, professional and contractor businesses pay $250, plus $15 per employee up to $300. It is billed with your business license. The area runs from Junipero Ave. to Pacific Coast Hwy. and 11th St. to 14th St.

Why it matters: Businesses along E. Anaheim St. in Zaferia: this is the fee added to your business license each year. The hearing is your chance to comment before Council confirms it.

Source: City Council staff report, Oct 20, 2026 (item 9)
